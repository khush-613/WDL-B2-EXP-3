About the Urine Test Strip Reader System

Objectives

The main objective is to give a fast and low cost urine test at the point of care. It also reduces the manual error which happens when a technician reads the strip colour with his own eyes.


Working Principle

The strip is dipped in the urine sample. Each reagent pad changes its colour. The calorimeter sends light on the pad and measures the reflected light. Then the system compares the colour with the standard chart and gives the value in mg/dL.


Benefits

Result comes in 60 seconds, no need of big laboratory, and the report can be given to the patient on the same day. It is useful in villages where there is no big hospital.


Message from the Doctor

Early testing of urine can find diabetes, kidney disease and infection at an early stage. Every patient above 30 years should do this test once in a year.
- Dr. Mehta, Pathology Department
