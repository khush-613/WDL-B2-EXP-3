Testing Procedure

  1. Collect the urine sample in a clean container
  2. Dip the test strip in the sample
  3. Remove the extra urine from the strip
  4. Put the strip in the reader machine
  5. Wait for 60 seconds
  6. Take the printed report

Users of the System
