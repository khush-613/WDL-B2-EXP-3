Urine Test Strip Reader - Test Information

What is the Urine Test Strip Reader ?

The Urine Test Strip Reader is a machine which reads the colour of the reagent pads on a urine strip. The strip is dipped in the urine sample and after some time the colour of the pads change. The calorimeter reads these colours and gives the result.

Important Test Parameters

The main parameters are Glucose, Protein and Ketones. High value of Glucose may show diabetes and high Protein may show kidney problem.

Instructions

Always collect the mid stream urine sample in a clean container.
Do not keep the strip in urine for more than 2 seconds.
Read the result only after 60 seconds.

Units and Symbols

Glucose is measured in mg/dL and 1 dL = 10-1 L.
The chemical formula of water is H2O and the acid used in the reagent pad is H2SO4.
Normal glucose range is 70 - 110 mg/dL and it is written as 102 mg/dL also.

Medical Abbreviations

UTI, RBC, WBC, pH and SG are used in the report.

Disclaimer : This report is only for screening purpose. Please consult a doctor before taking any medicine.

Old value 90 mg/dL is now updated to 120 mg/dL.
